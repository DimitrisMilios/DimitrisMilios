## 🧑‍💻 About me

- 📱 **Flutter developer at [SmartUp](https://smartupweb.com)**, a product studio in Thessaloniki. I build and ship production iOS and Android apps from a single codebase, from wireframe to store listing.
- 🛒 **Web & e-shops.** Business websites and B2B stores on WordPress and WooCommerce, with custom themes rather than off-the-shelf templates.
- 🔌 **IoT after hours.** I design and hand-solder my own connected hardware (ESP32, e-ink, OLED), write the firmware and the Flutter companion apps that talk to it over BLE and Wi-Fi.
- 🎓 International Hellenic University graduate, based in **Thessaloniki, Greece** 🇬🇷
- ☕ Fuel: infinite coffee.

## 🚀 What I'm building now

- **[Mokuply](https://github.com/DimitrisMilios/Mokuply)** – a free, open-source, 100% client-side studio for App Store and Google Play screenshots. Flutter Web, one shared canvas for both stores, realistic phone frames, Google Fonts, lossless PNG export. Nothing you upload leaves your machine.
- **[Veldash](https://github.com/DimitrisMilios/veldash)** – an ultra-light offline/online navigation app for low-spec aftermarket Android head units (1 GB RAM, Android 5+). Native Kotlin, MapLibre, bundled vector maps and BRouter routing, no Play Services.
